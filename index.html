What’s included in residential cleaning?

Your everyday essentials: accessible surfaces dusted and wiped, floors vacuumed and mopped, bathrooms cleaned, kitchen counters and appliance exteriors wiped, and bins emptied. Open the residential service card for the full checklist. Interior appliances and windows can be added separately.

Do you clean commercial spaces?

Yes. Commercial care can be tailored for offices, studios, small retail spaces, and shared work areas. Timing, access, floor area, washrooms, and high-touch priorities are confirmed before final pricing.

How much will my cleaning cost?

It depends on your space’s size, condition, service, and frequency. Our calculator gives an illustrative range in Canadian dollars to help you plan. It isn’t a binding quote; the team must confirm the scope, final price, applicable tax, and availability.

What’s included in an Airbnb turnover?

Turnovers focus on cleaning and presenting the property for the next guest: kitchens, bathrooms, surfaces, floors, beds, and a visual supply check. Linen handling, laundry, consumable restocking, and damage reporting should be agreed in your custom scope.

Can every carpet or stain be treated?

Carpet fibre, age, backing, previous treatments, and the type of spot all affect the right method and likely result. The team should inspect or discuss these details first; complete stain removal cannot be guaranteed.

Does getting an estimate book a visit?

No. The calculator lets you explore options and download a planning estimate. Nothing is sent, no payment is taken, and no appointment is reserved. Availability, scope, and final pricing must be confirmed separately with the team.
