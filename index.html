What’s included in residential cleaning?

Your everyday essentials: accessible surfaces dusted and wiped, floors vacuumed and mopped, bathrooms cleaned, kitchen counters and appliance exteriors wiped, and bins emptied. Open the residential service card for the full checklist. Interior appliances and windows can be discussed when confirming your scope.

Do you clean commercial spaces?

Yes. Commercial care can be tailored for offices, studios, small retail spaces, and shared work areas. Timing, access, floor area, washrooms, and high-touch priorities are confirmed before final pricing.

How much will my cleaning cost?

Standard cleaning services are $35 CAD per hour for one cleaner. Post-construction cleaning is $40 CAD per hour for one cleaner. The final total depends on the amount of work, the condition of the space, and the time required. Applicable tax, scope, and anticipated hours are confirmed before booking.

What’s included in an Airbnb turnover?

Turnovers focus on cleaning and presenting the property for the next guest: kitchens, bathrooms, surfaces, floors, beds, and a visual supply check. Linen handling, laundry, consumable restocking, and damage reporting should be agreed in your custom scope.

Can every carpet or stain be treated?

Carpet fibre, age, backing, previous treatments, and the type of spot all affect the right method and likely result. The team should inspect or discuss these details first; complete stain removal cannot be guaranteed.

Does viewing a rate book a visit?

No. The rate tool lets you explore services and download a rate summary. Nothing is sent, no payment is taken, and no appointment is reserved. Availability, workload, anticipated hours, and final pricing must be confirmed separately with the team.
